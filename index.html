Evolution

An evolving musical painting

Works of art are usually fixed in time by the artist. Here the artwork is a landscape of possibilities: twelve double-sided panels, rectangular or square, that can be turned over, turned round and rearranged into a vast number of compositions. Each visit is a different journey through it.

Journeys can be random, or evolutionary: a genetic algorithm searches for arrangements where colour and pattern flow across the edges between panels.
